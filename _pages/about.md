I am a postdoctoral researcher within the [Fisheries and Marine Ecosystem Model Intercomparison Project (FishMIP)](https://fishmip.org/) at the Foundation for Biodiversity Research’s Centre for the Synthesis and Analysis of Biodiversity (FRB-CESAB). My primary research interest is modelling social-ecological systems under global change, with a particular focus on the societal risks of climate-driven ecosystem change.

I’m currently working on FishMIP’s Ocean System Pathways project [FishMIP-OSP](https://www.fondationbiodiversite.fr/en/the-frb-in-action/programs-and-projects/le-cesab/fishmip_osp/) that is bringing together an ensemble of fisheries socio-ecological simulations to improve the evidence available for risk-informed marine governance. Previously, I completed my PhD research at the University of Plymouth under the supervision of Prof Abigail McQuatters-Gollop. My PhD research was co-funded by the UK Department for Food, Environment, and Rural Affairs (Defra) to assess the ecosystem service impacts of long-term ocean warming.
